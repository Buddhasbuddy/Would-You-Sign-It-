Copilot Said It — Would You Sign Your Name to It?

A FACTS Reflection for Out-of-Scope Leaders

Copilot can sound clear, professional and completely confident. It may also cite a policy, collective agreement, procedure, or other source. Neither confidence nor a citation guarantees that the conclusion is correct. For each case, decide whether you would be prepared to put your name behind Copilot's response.

What “Sign It” means: The response is appropriately bounded, supported, and suitable to continue using after normal human review. It does not mean the response is automatically correct or exempt from verification.
Assume approved use: Unless a scenario says otherwise, assume the information is being used in Saskatchewan Polytechnic's approved Copilot environment and that the user is authorized to work with it.
